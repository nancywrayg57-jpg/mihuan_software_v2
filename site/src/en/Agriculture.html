Agriculture

Digital Agriculture Integrated Management System

Full-Chain Smart Agriculture Control Platform Driven by IoT + AI

Providing integrated digital solutions from production monitoring, environmental regulation, quality traceability to supply chain management for modern agricultural industrial parks, planting bases and agriculture-related enterprises.

View six core functions Back to Products

Functions

Six Core Functions

01

IoT Data Collection

Real-time access to multi-dimensional sensors such as soil temperature and humidity, light, CO₂ and weather stations

02

AI Intelligent Decision-Making

Irrigation, fertilization, pest and disease warning based on crop growth models, yield prediction

03

Production Process Management

Full-process digitalization of planting plans, farming records and input management

04

Quality Traceability System

One item one code, full-chain traceability from field to table

05

Warehousing & Supply Chain

Intelligent inventory management, cold chain temperature and humidity monitoring, order and delivery coordination

06

Visual Data Dashboard

One-screen overview of overall park operation status, multi-level drill-down

Scenarios

Applicable Scenarios

Modern agricultural industrial parks, large-scale planting bases, government smart supervision platforms, fresh food brand traceability systems, agricultural research institutes

Customer Value

Cost reduction (15%-30% less water, fertilizer and pesticides), efficiency improvement (40% lower labor costs), quality improvement (standardized production), value addition (traceability empowers brand premium)
