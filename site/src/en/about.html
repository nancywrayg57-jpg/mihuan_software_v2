Company Profile

Company Profile

Guangzhou Honey Badger Software Co., Ltd. was founded on January 25, 2017. Rooted in Guangzhou's digital technology and innovation hub, the company has long focused on software development, information system integration, data processing and storage, intelligent tool R&D, and cross-border network server resource services. It is a comprehensive software technology company combining industry digitalization solutions, self-developed AI tools, and cross-border expansion support services.

Since its founding, the company has built on its self-developed technology foundation to form 4 regular products/services around digital agriculture, cross-border AI image tools, AI development training, and overseas social media operations, while also providing cross-border network services including static residential IP, datacenter IP, and dynamic IP. This scope serves both domestic industrial digitalization upgrades and the global expansion needs of Chinese companies. We adhere to self-developed technology, compliant operations, and implementation-first delivery, serving government and enterprise clients, agricultural bases, cross-border e-commerce sellers, overseas MCN teams, data technology service providers, and other customer groups with practical, adaptable, one-stop digital software and hardware support solutions.

Scope

Product and Service Scope (4 Regular Products/Services + Cross-border Network Services)

Based on years of software development and data service experience, the company has built four core business systems. Its external product scope is unified as 4 regular products/services + cross-border network services (including static residential IP, datacenter IP, and dynamic IP), covering the core needs of domestic industrial digitalization and cross-border expansion scenarios, and providing tailored digital solutions for customers across different industries.

01

Block 1: Agricultural Digitalization Solutions

Representative Product: Digital Agriculture Integrated Management System

For local agricultural cooperatives, large-scale planting bases, and agricultural regulatory organizations, this solution integrates IoT hardware, AI-powered monitoring, and cloud management platforms to digitize and intelligently upgrade the full process of agricultural production, field supervision, production and sales traceability, and farming workflow management. It helps replace traditional extensive agriculture operations with digital technology, supporting rural agricultural modernization, quality improvement, efficiency gains, and compliant management.

02

Block 2: Cross-border AI Self-developed Tools and Technical Training

Includes two core products: Honey Badger Original Image and AI-FDE VibeCoding Training.

Honey Badger Original Image: An AI image originality tool tailored for cross-border e-commerce. It can batch-generate compliant, highly original product materials, automatically reduce duplicate-check and risk-control exposure across overseas platforms, and adapt to Amazon, independent sites, TikTok Shop, and other global e-commerce scenarios, significantly reducing design costs and improving listing efficiency for cross-border sellers.

AI-FDE VibeCoding Training: A practical frontier AI natural-language development course for technical practitioners, cross-border entrepreneurs, and software development enthusiasts. The course focuses on commercial AI system implementation, teaching project development, scenario adaptation, and feature iteration step by step to cultivate practical AI development talent and help learners rapidly deliver commercial AI projects.

03

Block 3: Full-process Cross-border Social Media Private Domain Coaching

Representative Service: Social Commerce Private Domain Coaching

Focused on TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, and other major overseas social platforms, this service provides end-to-end practical coaching for overseas sellers, cross-border studios, and MCN organizations. It covers account cold start, environment setup, public traffic acquisition, private domain user retention, product conversion, monetization, and account risk-control avoidance, solving common pain points such as difficult overseas traffic operations, frequent account bans, and low conversion efficiency.

04

Block 4: Cross-border Full-scope Network Server Services

The company integrates three overseas network node product lines: static residential servers, datacenter servers, and dynamic residential servers, building a globally distributed cross-border server cluster to provide one-stop, full-scenario, highly compliant overseas network environment solutions for outbound businesses and cross-border practitioners, adapting to different risk-control standards and operational needs.

Static Residential Servers: Long-term fixed network nodes built on real overseas household ISP broadband, with native residential network attributes, low platform risk-control recognition, and stable dedicated environments. They are suitable for long-term cross-border store operations, overseas social media account matrices, cross-border payment account management, and stable long-term overseas platform login scenarios.

Datacenter Servers: Dedicated network nodes based on global professional backbone datacenters, focused on high bandwidth, low latency, and high concurrency. With efficient and stable transmission and strong cost performance, they are widely applicable to high-frequency batch scenarios such as large-scale market data collection, website performance stress testing, overseas business server deployment, short-term batch account registration, and batch ad verification.

Dynamic Residential Servers: Massive overseas 4G/5G carrier and household dynamic network pools that support automatic rotation by request, session, or timing. They can simulate natural mobile user access behavior, reduce platform blocking and traffic limitation risks, and support overseas short-video matrix operations, mobile app testing, local-life data collection, large-scale crawler data collection, ad effectiveness verification, and other high-frequency variable scenarios.

All cross-border network server services are paired with a visual unified management console and open APIs, supporting batch management, automated scheduling, real-time node monitoring, easy operation for beginners, and enterprise-scale automated batch operations across outbound business scenarios.

Competitiveness

Core Competitiveness

Honey Badger builds long-term competitiveness through self-developed technology, a dual-track business layout, a complete service loop, and compliant operations.

1. Full-chain Self-developed Technical Capabilities

Software development is the company's foundation. The underlying systems of its product lines are self-developed, covering agricultural IoT management platforms, AI image generation algorithms, global cross-border network server intelligent scheduling systems, AI development teaching frameworks, and other core technologies. The company does not rely on third-party base architectures and can rapidly iterate and customize according to customer needs, with strong technical autonomy and adaptability.

2. Differentiated Dual-track Business Layout

As a software company that simultaneously covers domestic smart agriculture and cross-border expansion, Honey Badger can support government and enterprise agricultural digitalization projects and rural industry upgrades, while also providing lightweight tools, compliant network environments, and implementation-oriented operations services for small and medium outbound sellers and cross-border enterprises. This diversified business structure provides stronger risk resistance and market adaptability than single-track companies.

3. Complete Product and Service Closed Loop

The company addresses the common industry problem of disconnected product sales and services. Beyond software and network server resource delivery, it provides implementation guidance, technical training, operations coaching, and 7x24 online operations support, following customers from product delivery and scenario adaptation through business implementation to solve the practical problem of "not knowing how to use it or not using it well after purchase."

4. Compliant and Stable Technical Operations System

The company strictly follows domestic laws and regulations on cybersecurity and data management. Its overseas cross-border network server node resources are operated compliantly throughout the process. Honey Badger continuously tracks risk-control rules of major cross-border platforms and AI industry technical standards, dynamically optimizing product algorithms and network scheduling logic to reduce customers' operational violation and blocking risks and support long-term business operations.
